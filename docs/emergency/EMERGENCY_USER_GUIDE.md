# ASRTAVA Emergency Department & Trauma User Guide

## 1. For Triage Nurses
1. **Initial Assessment (Within 3 Minutes of Arrival)**:
   - Identify patient (or click **Issue Temp Emergency UHID** if patient is unconscious / unidentified).
   - Enter vital signs (HR, BP, RR, SpO2, GCS, Pain score 0-10).
   - The system automatically assigns the ESI Level (1 to 5) and assigns the patient to the Red, Yellow, or Green bay.
2. **Immediate Red Zone Protocol**:
   - Any patient with $GCS < 9$, severe respiratory failure, or pulseless arrest is routed directly to Red Bay 1.

## 2. For Emergency & Trauma Physicians
1. **Activating Trauma Team Broadcast**:
   - For multi-system trauma, click **Broadcast Code Red / Trauma Alert**.
   - An alert immediately summons the trauma surgeon, anaesthetist, orthopaedic surgeon, and blood bank team.
2. **Placing STAT Diagnostic Overrides**:
   - Under the *STAT Emergency Overrides* tab, select necessary imaging (Polytrauma CT, FAST scan), labs (ABG, cardiac troponin), or blood products (MTP pack).
   - The orders bypass cashier clearance immediately.
3. **Documenting ATLS Resuscitation**:
   - Complete the Primary Survey checklist (Airway, Breathing, Circulation, Disability, Exposure).
4. **Final Disposition**:
   - When stabilization is achieved, transition the patient to the Emergency OT, ICU, Inpatient Ward, or discharge home.

## 3. For Ambulance Paramedics
1. **Dispatch & En-Route**:
   - Accept the 108/911 call assignment on the mobile unit.
   - GPS telemetry provides live ETA updates to the hospital.
2. **Mobile PCR Submission**:
   - Document on-scene vitals, IV fluid boluses, and oxygen delivery.
   - Synchronize the PCR with the ED receiving room before arrival at hospital bay.

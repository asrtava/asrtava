# ASRTAVA Emergency Triage & Acuity Classification Validation

## 1. Triage Methodology (Emergency Severity Index - ESI 5-Level)
ASRTAVA adopts the internationally validated Emergency Severity Index (ESI) 5-level algorithm for clinical decision-support at the emergency triage desk.

### Level 1: Resuscitation (Immediate Life Threat)
- **Clinical Presentation**: Cardiac or respiratory arrest, severe hypoxemia ($SpO_2 < 85\%$), profound shock, unresponsiveness ($GCS < 9$).
- **Action**: Immediate transfer to Red Resuscitation Bay with zero administrative delays.

### Level 2: Emergent (High Risk / Severe Distress)
- **Clinical Presentation**: Severe retrosternal chest pain (acute coronary syndrome), severe respiratory distress, acute focal neurological deficit (acute stroke), acute severe pain score ($\ge 8/10$).
- **Action**: Transfer to Yellow Acute Care Bay with attending physician evaluation within 10-15 minutes.

### Level 3: Urgent (Stable Vitals, Multiple Projected Resources)
- **Clinical Presentation**: Moderate abdominal pain, non-critical fractures, dehydration needing IV rehydration and serial laboratory/imaging evaluations.
- **Action**: Monitored in Yellow Urgent Observation area.

### Level 4: Less Urgent (Stable, Single Resource Required)
- **Clinical Presentation**: Simple laceration requiring sutures, mild localized sprain, uncomplicated urinary tract infection.
- **Action**: Green Zone Fast-Track Clinic.

### Level 5: Non-Urgent (No Diagnostic Resources Required)
- **Clinical Presentation**: Medication refill, suture removal, minor chronic rash.
- **Action**: Fast-track consultation or outpatient referral.

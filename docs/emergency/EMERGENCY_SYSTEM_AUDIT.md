# ASRTAVA Emergency Department & Critical Care System Audit

## 1. Executive Summary
The Emergency Department (ED) is the acute front door of any healthcare facility. Emergency care is characterized by time-critical decision making, incomplete patient demographic history, high clinical volatility, and life-threatening physiological crises.

## 2. Core Operational Deficiencies in Legacy Systems
- **Registration Bottlenecks**: Inability to register unconscious, trauma, or unidentified patients ("John Doe") immediately, causing catastrophic delays in emergency laboratory and blood bank orders.
- **Subjective Triage**: Lack of standardized triage algorithms (ESI 5-level / Manchester Triage), resulting in delayed prioritization of high-acuity patients.
- **Administrative Payment Blockades**: Software systems that halt STAT imaging, blood bank dispensing, or ICU beds pending cashier payment or insurance authorization.
- **Pre-Hospital Blind Spots**: Ambulance teams unable to transmit pre-arrival patient condition and vital signs to the emergency receiving room.
- **Inadequate Medico-Legal Traceability**: Loose documentation of Medico-Legal Cases (MLC), police intimations, and assault/accident injury declarations.

## 3. ASRTAVA Emergency Mandates
1. Instant Emergency Temporary UHID generation for unconscious / unknown patients without billing gates.
2. Standardized ESI 5-level digital triage engine with automated red/yellow/green bay assignment.
3. Zero-delay clinical exemption: All emergency resuscitation, blood transfusion, and surgical orders proceed immediately without cashier clearance.
4. Ambulance GPS fleet dispatch with pre-hospital Mobile PCR tele-transmission.
5. Medico-Legal Case (MLC) audit trail with automated police intimation logging.

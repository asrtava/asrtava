# ASRTAVA Trauma Care & ATLS Resuscitation Validation

## 1. Advanced Trauma Life Support (ATLS) Primary Survey
The primary survey systematically identifies and manages immediate life threats in order of clinical lethality:
1. **A — Airway with Cervical Spine Protection**:
   - Assessment of patency and foreign bodies.
   - Mandatory rigid cervical collar and in-line stabilization.
   - Immediate definitive airway (Endotracheal tube ETT) indicated for $GCS \le 8$ or severe facial burns/edema.
2. **B — Breathing & Ventilation**:
   - Immediate diagnosis of tension pneumothorax, open pneumothorax, flail chest, and massive hemothorax.
   - Rapid tube thoracostomy (Intercostal drain ICD) placement.
3. **C — Circulation with Hemorrhage Control**:
   - External hemorrhage compression / tourniquet application.
   - Two large-bore (14G / 16G) peripheral intravenous access or intraosseous (IO) lines.
   - Pelvic binder placement in suspected pelvic ring disruption.
   - FAST Ultrasound (Focused Assessment with Sonography for Trauma).
4. **D — Disability (Neurological Evaluation)**:
   - Glasgow Coma Scale (GCS, Eye 1-4, Verbal 1-5, Motor 1-6).
   - Pupil size, symmetry, and light reflex.
5. **E — Exposure & Environmental Control**:
   - Complete undressing for full trauma inspection followed immediately by hypothermia prevention blankets (warm IV fluids and forced-air warming).

## 2. Massive Transfusion Protocol (MTP)
- Triggered for severe hemorrhagic shock (Shock Index $\text{HR} / \text{Systolic BP} > 1.0$).
- Dispenses balanced 1:1:1 ratio (Packed Red Blood Cells : Fresh Frozen Plasma : Platelets) with intravenous Tranexamic Acid (TXA 1g IV bolus) within 3 hours of injury.

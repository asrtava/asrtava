# ASRTAVA Enterprise Analytics & BI User Guide

## 1. Executive Portal Access
1. Log in to ASRTAVA Web Portal with Executive, Hospital Administrator, or Board Member role.
2. Select **Domain 16: Reports & BI**.
3. View high-level KPI cards: ALOS, Bed Occupancy, AR Days, Clean Claim Rate, Readmissions, Mortality.

## 2. Using Predictive AI Models
- **LACE+ Calculator**: Enter patient's length of stay, acuity, comorbidities, and recent ED presentations. The model computes the score. If score $\ge 10$, a transition-of-care coordinator is alerted for 48h post-discharge monitoring.
- **No-Show Classifier**: When scheduling appointments with high lead times (e.g. > 14 days), the classifier flags appointments at high risk of patient absence, triggering automatic WhatsApp nudges and enabling double-booking slots.
- **Bed Shortage Forecasting**: Review 24-48h bed occupancy projections to adjust elective surgery admission schedules before physical ward gridlocks occur.

## 3. Scheduled BI Reports
- Automated reports can be configured for daily, weekly, or monthly delivery in PDF, CSV, or Excel format to executive email distribution lists.

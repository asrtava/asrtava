# Executive C-Suite Dashboard Specification

## 1. Executive Audience
Designed for Hospital CEO, Chief Medical Officer (CMO), Chief Operating Officer (COO), Chief Financial Officer (CFO), and Board of Directors.

## 2. Key Dashboard Metrics

### Financial & Operational Health
- **EBITDA & Operating Margin**: Real-time revenue vs operating expenses.
- **Gross & Net Revenue**: Segmented by Cash/Digital counter receipts vs Corporate/TPA receivables.
- **AR Days**: Days in Accounts Receivable tracked against benchmark (< 45 days).
- **Clean Claim Rate**: First-pass claims acceptance rate (> 95% target).

### Clinical Quality & Inpatient Flow
- **Average Length of Stay (ALOS)**: Monitored department-wise and compared against national DRG benchmarks.
- **Bed Occupancy Rate (BOR)**: Optimal target band 80-85%. Above 90% triggers automated step-down bed transfers.
- **30-Day Readmission Rate**: Evaluated against NABH benchmark (< 6.0%).
- **Inpatient Mortality Rate**: Segmented into early (< 48 hours) and late (> 48 hours).
- **Emergency Door-to-Doctor Time**: Averaged in real-time across all 5 triage levels.

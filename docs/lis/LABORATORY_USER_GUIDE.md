# ASRTAVA Laboratory Information System (LIS) User Manual & SOP

## 1. Phlebotomist & Accessioning Workflow

1. **Accessioning Worklist:** Open the Accessioning tab -> Scan the patient wristband.
2. **Container Selection:** Verify the required container tube (e.g. Lavender EDTA for CBC, Gold SST for Biochemistry).
3. **Print Barcode Label:** Affix barcode label immediately to the tube at the bedside or collection station.
4. **Transport:** Deliver samples to the central laboratory reception.

---

## 2. Laboratory Technologist Testing & Technical Validation

1. **Specimen Inspection:** Inspect tubes for hemolysis, clotting, or insufficient volume. If unacceptable, click **Reject Sample** and select the root cause.
2. **Analyzer Loading:** Load barcode-labeled tubes into the analyzer rack (Sysmex / Roche Cobas).
3. **Review Results:** Review the automated telemetry results staging queue. Inspect values against normal limits.
4. **Technical Sign:** Click **Technologist Validate** to advance the result to the pathologist queue.

---

## 3. Pathologist Medical Authorization & Critical Alert Handling

1. **Medical Review:** Review abnormal and critical results in the Pathologist Sign-Off Gate.
2. **Authorize Report:** Review clinical diagnosis and click **Authorize & Apply Digital Seal**. The report is released immediately to the patient and ordering doctor.
3. **Panic Values:** When a critical value alert is triggered, call the attending physician immediately and log the telephone read-back confirmation.

---

## 4. Internal Quality Control (IQC) Management

1. Prior to testing patient samples each morning, run Level 1, Level 2, and Level 3 control materials.
2. Enter measured values in the Westgard IQC tab.
3. If an out-of-control violation ($1_{3s}$ or $2_{2s}$) occurs, withhold patient testing, recalibrate the analyzer, and repeat the control run.

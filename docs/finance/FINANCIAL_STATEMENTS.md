# ASRTAVA Real-Time Financial Statements & Reporting

## 1. Live Trial Balance Statement

The ASRTAVA Trial Balance aggregates all debit balances and credit balances across the Chart of Accounts in real-time.

### 1.1 Balance Invariant
$$\sum \text{Debit Balances} = \sum \text{Credit Balances}$$

Any variance $> ₹0.00$ triggers an immediate reconciliation flag on the Finance Director's dashboard.

## 2. Profit & Loss (P&L) Statement

The Profit & Loss statement reflects the hospital's operational performance over any selected period:

$$\text{Net Operating Income} = \text{Total Operating Revenues} - \text{Total Operating Expenses}$$

### 2.1 Departmental Revenue Breakdown
- Outpatient Department (OPD) Consultations
- Inpatient Room & Nursing Care
- Operation Theatre & Surgical Packages
- Laboratory Diagnostics
- Radiology & Imaging
- Pharmacy Sales
- Emergency & Trauma Care

### 2.2 Operating Expense Breakdown
- Clinical & Surgical Consumables
- Doctor & Staff Salaries
- Utility & Facility Maintenance
- Contractual Deductions & Patient Discounts

---

## 3. Balance Sheet

The Balance Sheet organizes hospital financial health into:
1. **Assets**: Cash in Hand, Bank Accounts, Accounts Receivable (OPD, IPD, TPA, Corporate), Pharmaceutical Inventory, Medical Equipment & Fixed Assets.
2. **Liabilities**: Trade Payables (Vendors), Output GST Payable, Patient Advance Deposits, Statutory Deductions (TDS, PF, ESI).
3. **Equity**: Initial Hospital Capital, Retained Earnings, Current Year Operating Surplus.

# ASRTAVA Centralized Group Reporting & Business Intelligence

## 1. Overview

The Centralized Group Reporting Engine synthesizes clinical, operational, financial, and inventory data across all hospitals and branches within a healthcare group into real-time business intelligence reports.

---

## 2. Standard Enterprise Group Reports

| Report Code | Title | Core Dimensions | Metrics Captured |
|---|---|---|---|
| `REP-GRP-001` | Multi-Hospital Operational Overview | Hospital, Region, Facility Type | Total active staff, OPD consults, IPD census, emergency triage volume |
| `REP-GRP-002` | Bed Occupancy & Capacity Utilization | Wards, Bed Categories (ICU/General) | Total beds, occupied beds, occupancy percentage, ALOS (Average Length of Stay) |
| `REP-GRP-003` | Consolidated Revenue & EBITDA Rollup | Legal Entity, Revenue Department | Gross revenue, discounts, net revenue, GST collection, outstanding TPA claims |
| `REP-GRP-004` | Enterprise Master Patient Flow | City, Acquisition Channel, Specialty | New vs returning patients, cross-facility visits, EMPI growth rate |
| `REP-GRP-005` | Inter-Branch Stock & Formulary Audit | Drug Molecule, Warehouse, Expiry | Near-expiry batches (< 90 days), inter-branch stock movement, transfer duration |
| `REP-GRP-006` | Shared Specialist Productivity | Doctor, Speciality, Branch | Workplaces visited, consult volume, surgical cases, schedule adherence |

---

## 3. Privacy-Preserving Aggregation

In strict accordance with the DPDP Act 2023:
- Business intelligence exports do not serialize patient identifiable attributes (Aadhaar, home address, phone numbers) unless generated by an authorized hospital medical records officer for accredited clinical audits.
- Export formats supported: Encrypted CSV, PDF Executive Summaries, and Scheduled SFTP/S3 JSON dumps.

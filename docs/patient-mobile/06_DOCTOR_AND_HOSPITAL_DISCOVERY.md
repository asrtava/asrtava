# Doctor & Hospital Discovery

## 1. Search & Filtering Engine
Patients can discover and review verified hospital medical faculty (`DoctorDiscoveryScreen.tsx`):
- Real-time text search by doctor name, specialty, or clinical department.
- Specialty filter pills: Cardiology, General Medicine, Orthopedics, Pediatrics, Neurology.
- Doctor Cards displaying:
  - Doctor Name & Medical Qualifications (e.g. *MBBS, MD, DM (Cardiology), FACC*).
  - Years of Clinical Experience.
  - Transparent Consultation Fee (e.g. *₹ 800.00*).
  - Next Available Real-Time Slot.
  - 1-tap "Book Appointment" CTA button.

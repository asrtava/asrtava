# Inpatient Admissions & Discharge Summaries

## 1. Overview (`AdmissionsScreen.tsx`)
Enables patients and authorized family members to review hospital inpatient admission records:
- Facility, Ward Name (e.g. *Cardiac Care Unit / Step-Down*), Room & Bed number.
- Attending Senior Consultant.
- Admission Date & Initial Clinical Diagnosis.
- Course in Hospital & Treatment Summary: Comprehensive details of medications, procedures, and daily progress.
- Discharge Summary PDF: Instant export of formal discharge papers for insurance claims or home-care nursing.

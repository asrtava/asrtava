# Patient Consent & DPDP 2023 Privacy Compliance

## 1. Statutory Compliance (Digital Personal Data Protection Act)
ASRTAVA enforces strict consent management for patient data handling:
- **Explicit Informed Consent**: Captured during registration and hospital linking.
- **Consent Ledger**:
  - Unique Consent Reference (e.g. `#CON-88192`).
  - Active tenant organization context.
  - Specified purpose: Clinical care, diagnostic investigations, pharmacy dispensing, billing.
  - Timestamped immutable ledger in `patient_consents`.
- **Consent Withdrawal**: Patients can inspect their consent history and adjust non-essential communication permissions directly from their mobile device.

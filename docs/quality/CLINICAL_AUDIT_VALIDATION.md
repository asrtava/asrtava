# Clinical Audit & Peer Review Validation

## 1. Scope
Validation of clinical audits, prescription reviews, medical record completeness checks, surgical mortality/morbidity reviews, and departmental quality peer reviews.

## 2. Audit Workflow
1. **Audit Initiation**: Defines audit scope, criteria, standard reference (e.g., NABH COP.14), lead auditor, and sample size.
2. **Execution**: Evaluator scores individual items against an objective checklist (Compliant, Partially Compliant, Non-Compliant, N/A).
3. **Findings Generation**: Non-compliant items automatically spawn non-conformance records and CAPA requests.
4. **Follow-Up Audit**: Scheduled within 30-90 days to re-sample and confirm that corrective measures took effect.

## 3. Morbidity & Mortality (M&M) Peer Review
- Multi-disciplinary review for all inpatient deaths and unexpected surgical complications.
- Assessment criteria:
  - Preventability rating (Virtually unpreventable, Definitely preventable, Probably preventable).
  - System vs. individual contributory factors.
  - Action plan for clinical protocol updates.

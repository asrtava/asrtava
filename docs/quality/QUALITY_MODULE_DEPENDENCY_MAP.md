# ASRTAVA — Quality Module Dependency & Integration Map

## 1. Upstream Data Providers to Quality Management
```
[Emergency / OPD / IPD] ───► [Patient Safety Incident Ingest]
[Pharmacy Dispensing]   ───► [Medication Error & Adverse Drug Reaction (ADR)]
[Operation Theatre]     ───► [WHO Surgical Safety Checklist Audits & SSI Surveillance]
[ICU & Wards]           ───► [Device Exposure Days (Ventilator, Foley, Central Line)]
[Laboratory (LIS)]      ───► [Microbiology Positive Blood/Urine Cultures & Organisms]
[Biomedical Assets]     ───► [Equipment Breakdown Log & Calibration Deficiencies]
[Nursing Administration]───► [Morse Fall & Braden Pressure Ulcer Audits]
[HR & Training]         ───► [Mandatory Infection Control & Safety Training Records]
```

---

## 2. Downstream Outputs from Quality Management
- **Hospital Leadership**: Executive Quality Dashboards, Clinical Governance KPIs, Risk Matrix.
- **Accreditation Bodies (NABH / NABL)**: Clause-by-clause evidence dossiers, audit findings, and mock assessment readiness reports.
- **Department Heads**: Departmental incident investigation reports, pending CAPA action items, and hand hygiene compliance scores.
- **Patients & Grievances**: Patient complaint acknowledgement letters, investigation outcomes, and satisfaction corrective measures.

# Infection Prevention & Control (IPC) Validation

## 1. Scope
Surveillance of Healthcare-Associated Infections (HAIs), antimicrobial stewardship, hand hygiene compliance, and bio-medical waste segregation.

## 2. Standard HAI Rate Formulations
Calculated normalized per 1,000 device exposure days:
- **CAUTI (Catheter-Associated Urinary Tract Infection)**:
  $$\text{CAUTI Rate} = \frac{\text{Number of CAUTIs}}{\text{Total Urinary Catheter Days}} \times 1,000$$
- **CLABSI (Central Line-Associated Bloodstream Infection)**:
  $$\text{CLABSI Rate} = \frac{\text{Number of CLABSIs}}{\text{Total Central Line Days}} \times 1,000$$
- **VAP (Ventilator-Associated Pneumonia)**:
  $$\text{VAP Rate} = \frac{\text{Number of VAPs}}{\text{Total Mechanical Ventilator Days}} \times 1,000$$
- **SSI (Surgical Site Infection)**:
  $$\text{SSI Rate} = \frac{\text{Number of SSIs}}{\text{Total Operative Procedures}} \times 100$$

## 3. Hand Hygiene Compliance
- Structured on **WHO 5 Moments for Hand Hygiene**:
  1. Before touching a patient
  2. Before clean/aseptic procedure
  3. After body fluid exposure risk
  4. After touching a patient
  5. After touching patient surroundings
- Real-time compliance logging per ward, nursing shift, and clinician cadre.

## 4. Bio-Medical Waste (BMW) Management
- Compliance with Bio-Medical Waste Management Rules (2016).
- Segregated tracking: Yellow (anatomical/soiled), Red (contaminated plastics), White (translucent sharps), Blue (glassware/metallic implants).
- Barcode generation and waste handler weight verification manifests.

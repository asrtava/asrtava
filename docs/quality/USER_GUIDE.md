# ASRTAVA Quality Management & Accreditation User Guide

## 1. Quality Manager Workflow
1. **Accessing Quality Hub**: Navigate to Hospital Admin Portal → Implementation & Governance → `🛡️ Quality & Accreditation (NABH / CAPA)`.
2. **Reviewing Incident Queue**: Inspect new incidents submitted by staff across wards, ICU, and OT. Assign severity (Near Miss, Adverse, Sentinel).
3. **Conducting RCA**: Open incident, trigger 5-Whys analysis, document system vs. process root causes.
4. **Initiating CAPA**: Assign corrective action items to department in-charge with target completion dates.
5. **Verifying Effectiveness**: Once the department marks remediation completed, an independent auditor verifies the fix on the ground and transitions the item to `VERIFIED_CLOSED`.

## 2. Infection Control Nurse (ICN) Workflow
1. **Daily Device Surveillance**: Track active central lines, urinary catheters, and mechanical ventilators across ICUs.
2. **Record HAI Case**: Upon positive blood/urine/sputum culture matching CDC/NHSN criteria, record the infection. System computes the infection rate per 1,000 device days.
3. **Hand Hygiene Audits**: Perform unannounced observational rounds evaluating adherence to WHO 5 Moments across nursing and medical staff.

## 3. Accreditation Officer Workflow
1. **NABH Assessment**: Track live compliance percentages across all 10 NABH chapters.
2. **Document Control**: Review clinical SOPs, approve revisions, and verify that departmental staff have acknowledged reading updated guidelines.

# Patient Safety & Clinical Risk Governance Validation

## 1. Scope
Validation of clinical risk management, proactive risk register (5x5 matrix), medication error mitigation, fall prevention, and surgical safety protocols.

## 2. Clinical Risk Assessment Framework
- **Likelihood Scoring (1-5)**:
  1. Rare (< 5% probability)
  2. Unlikely (5-20%)
  3. Possible (21-50%)
  4. Likely (51-80%)
  5. Almost Certain (> 80%)
- **Impact Scoring (1-5)**:
  1. Negligible (no harm, minor discomfort)
  2. Minor (first aid, minimal delay)
  3. Moderate (temporary harm, extended stay)
  4. Major (permanent disability, major surgery)
  5. Catastrophic (death or permanent loss of function)
- **Risk Calculation**:
  $$\text{Inherent Risk} = \text{Likelihood} \times \text{Impact}$$
  $$\text{Residual Risk} = \text{Likelihood post-controls} \times \text{Impact post-controls}$$

## 3. High-Alert Medication Double Checks
- Dual-signoff required for chemotherapy, insulin infusions, concentrated electrolytes, and high-potency opioids.
- Real-time LASA (Look-Alike Sound-Alike) warnings displayed on clinician ordering and nurse administration screens.

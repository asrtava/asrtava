# Hospital Accreditation & Standards Validation (NABH & NABL)

## 1. Scope
Validation of hospital readiness against National Accreditation Board for Hospitals & Healthcare Providers (NABH 5th Edition) and National Accreditation Board for Testing and Calibration Laboratories (NABL ISO 15189:2022).

## 2. NABH 5th Edition Core Chapters Covered
- **AAC (Access, Assessment and Continuity of Care)**: Registration, triage, initial assessment timeframes, discharge summaries.
- **COP (Care of Patients)**: Uniform care, high-risk patients, emergency resuscitation, sedation and anaesthesia safety.
- **MOM (Management of Medication)**: Formularies, LASA drugs, cold chain maintenance, prescription audits.
- **PRE (Patient Rights and Education)**: Informed consent, grievance redressal, billing transparency.
- **HIC (Hospital Infection Control)**: Surveillance, isolation rooms, sterilisation assurance, PPE adherence.
- **PSQ (Patient Safety and Quality Improvement)**: Incident reporting, RCA, clinical indicators, risk register.
- **ROM (Responsibilities of Management)**: Legal compliance, governance framework.
- **FMS (Facility Management and Safety)**: Fire safety, equipment maintenance, bio-medical waste, disaster management.
- **HRM (Human Resource Management)**: Credentialing, privileging, CPR training compliance.
- **IMS (Information Management System)**: EMR confidentiality, backup redundancy, access controls.

## 3. Real-Time Readiness Calculation
Readiness scores are computed directly from operational system data (e.g. audit completions, incident closures, SOP read acknowledgments, equipment PM completion) rather than self-reported checkboxes.

# ASRTAVA Operation Theatre & Perioperative Care User Guide

## 1. For Surgeons
1. **Booking an Operative Case**:
   - Access the *Operation Theatre* workspace -> *Surgical Schedules*.
   - Click **Schedule New Case**, enter patient UHID, pre-op diagnosis, proposed surgery, and estimated operative time.
   - The conflict engine verifies surgeon, anaesthetist, and room availability before confirming the booking.
2. **Informed Consent**:
   - Ensure the surgical consent form is completed with disclosed risks and patient/guardian signatures.
3. **Conducting the WHO Time-Out**:
   - Immediately before skin incision, lead the formal Time-Out verbally with all theatre staff present.
   - Confirm patient name, operative procedure, anatomical site, and verify that prophylactic antibiotics were administered within the preceding 60 minutes.

## 2. For Anaesthetists
1. **Pre-Anaesthetic Checkup (PAC)**:
   - Record ASA score (I-VI) and Mallampati airway class.
   - Verify that solid food fasting compliance is $\ge 6$ hours.
2. **Phase 1: Sign-In**:
   - Complete the Sign-In checklist prior to administering anaesthetic induction.
3. **Intraoperative Monitoring**:
   - Record vital parameters and controlled narcotics in the digital anaesthesia record.

## 3. For Theatre & Scrub Nurses
1. **Surgical Counts**:
   - Count all radiopaque sponges, surgical gauze, suture sharps, and surgical instruments before incision.
   - Record any instruments added during surgery.
   - Perform closing counts prior to cavity closure. Both scrub nurse and circulating nurse must cosign.

## 4. For PACU Nurses
1. **Monitoring Recovery**:
   - Evaluate the 5 Modified Aldrete parameters every 15 minutes.
2. **Safe Ward Transfer**:
   - Do not discharge a patient to a general ward until the total score reaches 9 or higher.

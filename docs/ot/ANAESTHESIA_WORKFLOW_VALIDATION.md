# ASRTAVA Anaesthesia Workflow & Intraoperative Telemetry Validation

## 1. Anaesthetic Techniques Supported
- General Anaesthesia (Endotracheal Intubation ETT, Laryngeal Mask Airway LMA).
- Neuraxial Anaesthesia (Spinal Subarachnoid Block SAB, Epidural).
- Regional Peripheral Nerve Blocks (Ultrasound-guided brachial plexus, femoral, sciatic).
- Monitored Anaesthesia Care (MAC) with deep sedation.
- Local Infiltration.

## 2. Hemodynamic & Ventilation Monitoring
- Pulse rate, non-invasive / invasive arterial blood pressure (systolic, diastolic, mean arterial pressure MAP).
- Continuous pulse oximetry ($SpO_2$) and capnography ($EtCO_2$).
- Temperature monitoring via esophageal / nasopharyngeal probe.

## 3. Controlled Narcotics Accountability
- Strict digital accounting for intraoperative opioids, hypnotics, and neuromuscular blockers:
  - Fentanyl (micrograms), Morphine (milligrams).
  - Propofol, Etomidate, Ketamine.
  - Vecuronium, Rocuronium, Atracurium.
  - Reversal agents (Sugammadex, Neostigmine/Glycopyrrolate).
- Timestamped dose logs prevent discrepancies and ensure hospital pharmacy narcotic reconciliation.

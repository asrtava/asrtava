# Customer Handover & Go-Live Protocol

## 1. 18-Stage Onboarding & Go-Live Verification
All customer handovers must complete the 14 mandatory readiness gates before commercial production sign-off:
1. Executive Sponsorship & Project Charter Sign-off
2. Departmental Configuration & Tariff Master Ingestion
3. Doctor & Clinical Staff Credentialing
4. Patient Data Migration & Reconciliation Audit
5. User Acceptance Testing (UAT) with 0 Critical/High Defects
6. Staff Role-Specific Training Certification (Minimum 80% passing score)
7. Mock Go-Live Simulation & Disaster Recovery Drill
8. Formal Dual Sign-Off (Hospital Administrator + ASRTAVA Implementation Lead)
9. 30-Day Hypercare Support Window Activated

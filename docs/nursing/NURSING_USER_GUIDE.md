# ASRTAVA Nursing Staff Operational Manual & SOP

## 1. Shift Routine & Workload Management

1. **Shift Login:** Log in to the Nursing Portal -> Select your assigned Ward and Shift (Morning, Evening, Night).
2. **Review Allocations:** Inspect the Workload & Assignments tab to review assigned beds and acuity scores.
3. **Incoming Handover:** Open the SBAR Handover tab, review the outgoing shift summary, check pending tasks, and click **Countersign Handover**.

---

## 2. Bedside Patient Identification & Clinical Pass

1. **Approach Bedside:** Scan the 2D barcode on the patient's wristband. Verify name, UHID, and date of birth.
2. **Vitals Recording:** Input blood pressure, heart rate, respiratory rate, temperature, SpO2, and pain score. The system automatically computes the NEWS2 score.
3. **Abnormal Alerts:** If NEWS2 $\ge 5$, an urgent review banner appears. Acknowledge the alert and follow hospital escalation protocols.

---

## 3. Medication Administration (eMAR)

1. **Inspect Scheduled Passes:** Open the Bedside eMAR tab to identify due doses.
2. **Five Rights:** Verify Right Patient, Drug, Dose, Route, and Time.
3. **High-Alert Meds:** If insulin, heparin, or narcotics are administered, prompt a second registered nurse to inspect the syringe/vial and enter their badge credentials in the dual-cosign modal.
4. **Omission/Hold:** If holding medication (e.g. Digoxin for bradycardia), select the clinical hold reason.

---

## 4. Shift Handover (SBAR)

1. At the conclusion of your shift, open **SBAR Handover**.
2. Document Situation, Background, Assessment, and Recommendation.
3. Click **Submit & Sign Outgoing Handover**.
4. Hand over bedside report directly to the incoming nurse.

# ASRTAVA Operational Analytics Module

## 1. Overview
The Operational Analytics module tracks patient throughput, waiting times, and hospital asset utilization across the care continuum.

## 2. Key Operational Metrics
- **OPD Waiting Times**: Measures average elapsed time across care stages (Registration $\rightarrow$ Nursing Triage $\rightarrow$ Doctor Consultation). Target: $<35$ minutes total turnaround.
- **Peak Consultation Hours**: Identifies peak outpatient traffic windows ($10:00 - 12:00$ and $17:00 - 19:00$) to balance doctor and nursing shift staffing.
- **Bed Utilization Grid**: Tracks bed occupancy percentages across General Wards, Private Deluxe, Semi-Private, and ICU suites.
- **Booking Channels**: Analyzes proportion of appointments booked via Mobile App, Web Portal, WhatsApp, and Front-Desk Walk-in.

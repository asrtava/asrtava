# ASRTAVA Inventory and Procurement Analytics

## 1. Overview
The Inventory and Procurement Analytics module monitors hospital surgical consumables, medical supplies, reorder points, and supplier purchase orders.

## 2. Key Capabilities
- **Current Stock & Valuation**: Real-time asset value of all warehouse and departmental stores.
- **Automated Reorder Alerts**: Flags items reaching minimum safety buffer levels before stockouts occur.
- **Purchase Order Tracking**: Analyzes vendor fulfillment timeliness, purchase returns, and discrepancies between ordered vs delivered purchase invoices.
- **Consumption by Department**: Identifies surgical consumables consumption patterns in Operation Theatres (OT), Intensive Care Units (ICU), and Emergency.

# ASRTAVA SaaS Platform Business Intelligence

## 1. Overview
The SaaS Platform BI module provides executive telemetry for the ASRTAVA platform owner, isolated strictly from hospital tenant clinical and financial records.

## 2. Key SaaS Metrics
- **Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)**: Active monthly contracted subscription revenue (₹ 19.5 Lakhs).
- **Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR)**: $\text{MRR} \times 12$ (₹ 2.34 Crore).
- **Tenant Cohorts**: Breakdown across active hospitals (54), trial organizations (3), and suspended accounts (1).
- **Subscription Plan Distribution**: Breakdown by tiers:
  - Enterprise Tier: 14 hospitals (₹ 8.4 Lakhs MRR)
  - Professional Tier: 22 hospitals (₹ 6.6 Lakhs MRR)
  - Starter Tier: 18 hospitals (₹ 3.6 Lakhs MRR)
- **Monthly Churn Rate**: Percentage of cancelled subscriptions ($0.8\%$).
- **Trial-to-Paid Conversion Rate**: 78.5%.
- **Platform Cloud Storage**: 142.6 GB consumed across all tenant document vaults.

# ASRTAVA Clinical Analytics Module

## 1. Overview
The Clinical Analytics module extracts epidemiological and clinical patterns from authorized consultation encounters while preserving patient confidentiality through aggregation and de-identification.

## 2. Key Capabilities
- **ICD-10 Diagnosis Frequency**: Tracks prevalence of primary and secondary diagnoses (e.g. `I10` Hypertension, `E11.9` Type 2 Diabetes, `J06.9` Acute URI).
- **Department Visit Volumes**: Analyzes outpatient throughput across General Medicine, Cardiology, Orthopedics, Pediatrics, and OBGYN.
- **Follow-up Adherence**: Measures percentage of chronic care patients returning for scheduled reviews within the designated recall window.
- **Zero Raw PII Exposure**: Clinical dashboards present aggregate counts and percentages without revealing identifiable patient charts or names.

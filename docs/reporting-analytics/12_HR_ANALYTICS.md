# ASRTAVA Human Resources (HR) & Payroll Analytics

## 1. Overview
The HR Analytics module monitors hospital clinical and administrative staffing, shift allocations, daily attendance, leave utilization, and aggregated payroll expenses.

## 2. Key Capabilities
- **Departmental Headcount**: Tracks staff distribution across Nursing (42), Medical Officers (28), Administrative (18), Laboratory/Radiology (14), and Pharmacy (12).
- **Daily Attendance & Shift Coverage**: Real-time biometric attendance rates and nurse-to-patient staffing ratios.
- **Aggregated Payroll Ledgers**: High-level monthly salary expenses and statutory deductions without exposing individual confidential compensation details to unauthorized roles.

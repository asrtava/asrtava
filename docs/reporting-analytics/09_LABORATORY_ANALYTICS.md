# ASRTAVA Laboratory Analytics

## 1. Overview
The Laboratory Analytics module monitors pathology throughput, turnaround times (TAT), and critical alert frequencies across automated analyzer workloads.

## 2. Key Metrics
- **Average Turnaround Time (TAT)**: Calculated from specimen accessioning to pathologist digital verification (Target: $<3.0$ hours for routine; $<60$ minutes for STAT emergency orders).
- **Test Category Volume**: Breakdown across Complete Blood Counts (CBC), Biochemistry (LFT/KFT), Thyroid profiles, and Clinical Pathology.
- **Critical Panic Alerts**: Tracks incidence of panic values (e.g. Potassium $<2.5$ or $>6.5$, Platelets $<20,000$) immediately triggering high-priority nursing alerts.
- **Specimen Rejection Trends**: Monitors hemolyzed or clotted samples requiring recollect.

# ASRTAVA Radiology Analytics

## 1. Overview
The Radiology Analytics module provides diagnostic imaging volume metrics, reporting turnaround times, and equipment utilization across all modalities connected via DICOMweb.

## 2. Key Metrics
- **Modality Breakdown**: Categorizes imaging studies across:
  - `DX / CR`: Digital Radiography / X-Ray (55.9%)
  - `USG`: Ultrasound (23.5%)
  - `CT`: Computed Tomography (14.0%)
  - `MR`: Magnetic Resonance Imaging (6.6%)
- **Reporting Turnaround Time**: Measures elapsed time from technologist study completion to radiologist finalized impression report.
- **Department Referral Origins**: Analyzes which clinical departments generate the highest imaging volume (Orthopedics, Emergency, General Medicine).
